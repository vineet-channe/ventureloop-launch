Built for safety, priced for students

Three core systems make TutorGuard the only marketplace students, tutors, and universities can trust.

🛡️

Verification & Background Check Pipeline

Every tutor completes a mandatory onboarding flow: government ID liveness verification, degree/enrollment cross-check against registrar records, and a national + county-level background check re-run every 12 months or upon any safety flag. A 2-person Trust & Safety team manually reviews mismatches within 48 hours. The Verified badge only appears once all checks pass — unverified tutors cannot list in-person sessions.

Core Safety System
🚨

Safety & Trust Toolkit

Every in-person session is protected: required liability waiver before a first meeting, GPS-tagged check-in/check-out with automatic no-show flagging after 15 minutes, an in-app SOS button with location sharing, and $1M tutor liability micro-insurance included in the platform commission. All incidents auto-escalate to Trust & Safety within 1 hour.

Session Protection
🏫

Institutional Partner Dashboard

A dedicated dashboard gives university administrators visibility into aggregate tutor verification status, insurance certificates, data-sharing agreement status, and incident logs. Institutions can gate access to only verified-and-insured tutors, launch a co-branded landing page, and unlock bulk licensing pricing.

How TutorGuard works

Simple for students, rewarding for tutors, accountable for institutions.

Post or search a request
