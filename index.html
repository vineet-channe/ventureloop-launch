Why TutorVerified

Trust, Safety & Affordability — Built In

Every tutor on our platform passes a rigorous, multi-step verification process before they can ever meet a student in person.

Priority 1
🛡️

Verification & Background Check Pipeline

Government ID liveness checks, transcript/degree cross-verification via registrar APIs, national + county-level criminal background checks (re-run annually or on any safety flag), and manual Trust & Safety review within 48 hours. Only fully verified tutors earn the badge and can list in-person sessions.

Priority 2
🚨

Safety & Trust Toolkit

Required liability waivers, GPS check-in/check-out with 15-minute no-show auto-flagging, an in-app SOS button linked to campus security & 911, and mandatory $1M tutor liability micro-insurance for every in-person session — all incidents escalate to Trust & Safety within the hour.

Priority 3
🏛️

Institutional Partner Dashboard

A compliance center for university administrators: verification status, insurance certificates, FERPA-compliant data agreements, incident logs, and campus tutor pool stats — with a co-brandable landing page and bulk licensing options.

Transparent Pricing. Escrowed Protection.

Every session's payment is held in escrow until it's confirmed complete. We take a flat 15% platform commission — no surprise fees, no markup games. That commission also funds tutor liability insurance and 24/7 Trust & Safety coverage.
