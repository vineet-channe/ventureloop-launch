Built Around How Students Actually Study

Three core features designed to help you find help faster — without sacrificing trust or safety.

🎓

Two-Tier Verified Badge

Every tutor badge shows exactly what's been checked: enrollment or degree status confirmed through a clearinghouse-style verification, plus a separate academic performance review against a 3.0+ GPA benchmark in matched subjects, conducted by our internal team with ongoing random audits. You see which checks passed — not a vague "verified" label.

🔀

Choose Virtual or In-Person

You decide how to meet. Virtual sessions run on an encrypted video platform with optional recording and chat moderation. In-person sessions activate location-based matching and additional safety measures designed specifically for meeting face-to-face.

📍

On-Demand Matching

Need help in person, fast? We search within a 3-mile radius first, expanding by 2 miles every 5 minutes up to 15 miles until a tutor is found. Prefer virtual? Skip the wait — you're matched instantly by subject and availability.

Verification You Can See — Not Just a Badge

We don't hide behind a single checkmark. Because no outside database can confirm course-level grades, we break verification into distinct, visible steps so you know exactly what's been reviewed.

  • ✅ Enrollment or degree status confirmed via clearinghouse-style verification
  • ✅ Academic performance reviewed internally against a 3.0+ GPA benchmark, with random monthly audits requiring official transcripts
  • ✅ In-person tutors go through additional background screening before meeting students face-to-face
  • ✅ Students can add an emergency contact for in-person sessions

Why this matters

Academic credentials and safety needs aren't one-size-fits-all. That's why virtual and in-person sessions are verified differently — so the protections you get actually match how you're meeting your tutor.

We're building this transparently, and continuing to expand our safety protocols as the platform grows.
