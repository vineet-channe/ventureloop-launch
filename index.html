Built for trust, not just convenience

Every core feature is designed around one question: how do we verify what we claim, and how do we avoid overpromising on what we can't?

🎓

Two-Tier Verified Badge System

A tutor's "Verified" badge is built from two independent checks, shown separately so you know exactly what's been confirmed:

  • Enrollment check: Confirmed via a clearinghouse-style third-party API, which attests active enrollment or degree status only.
  • GPA check: Verified internally — tutors upload an unofficial transcript, reviewed by our verification team against a 3.0+ threshold in subject-matched courses.

Because no external API can confirm course-level GPA, this is a manual review process, reinforced by a random 10% monthly audit requiring an official registrar transcript.

✔ Enrollment  |  ✔ GPA — shown independently
🔀

Session Modality Selector (Virtual or In-Person)

At booking, you choose exactly how you want to meet — this choice determines which safety and matching systems apply:

  • Virtual: Encrypted video via Twilio Video, with optional recording and chat moderation. No geo-matching or physical safety protocol applies, since no in-person meeting occurs.
  • In-Person: Activates geo-radius matching, mandatory background screening tier, and a required emergency contact step before the booking can be confirmed.

This modality flag is stored on every session and drives which downstream protections are enforced.

📍

On-Demand Geo-Matching (In-Person Only)

Applies exclusively to in-person sessions. Virtual sessions are matched instantly by subject and availability, with no location constraint.

  • Starts with a 3-mile radius search.
  • Expands by 2 miles every 5 minutes if no match is found.
  • Caps at a 15-mile radius.

Radius expansion and check-in logic never apply to virtual bookings — they're two separate matching paths by design.

Safety measures, clearly scoped

We'd rather tell you exactly what's required than imply more than we can guarantee.

🛡️ Background screening for in-person tutors

Tutors offering in-person sessions must complete a mandatory background screening as part of verification. Screening provider, scope, and renewal cadence are disclosed in-app during onboarding and in our Trust & Safety policy — this is a required gate, not an optional add-on.

📞 Mandatory emergency contact for in-person sessions

Before an in-person booking can be confirmed, students must designate an emergency contact. This step cannot be skipped — the booking flow blocks confirmation until it's completed, so this safety layer is never optional.

🔍 Transparent verification labels

Badges show which specific checks a tutor has passed (enrollment, GPA) rather than a single unexplained "verified" stamp, so you can judge credibility for yourself.

🎥 Virtual-specific protections

Virtual sessions use encrypted video with optional recording and chat moderation — protections suited to an online interaction, distinct from in-person physical safety protocols.
