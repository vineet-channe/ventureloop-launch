Built for How Students Actually Study

Three core systems make sure every session is a good match — academically and logistically.

🎓

Two-Tier Verified Badge

A tutor's "Verified" badge shows exactly what's been checked. Enrollment status is confirmed through a clearinghouse-style API, which attests active enrollment or degree completion only. Academic performance is verified separately: our internal team reviews an uploaded unofficial transcript against a 3.0+ GPA benchmark for subject-matched courses, since no external database can confirm course-level GPA. To keep this honest, 10% of tutors are randomly audited each month using an official registrar-issued transcript. Badges display each sub-check individually — so you know exactly what's been confirmed, not just that a badge exists.

🔀

Session Modality Selector

At booking, you choose Virtual or In-Person — and that choice determines what happens next. Virtual sessions run on an encrypted video platform with optional recording and chat moderation, with no location data collected. In-Person sessions activate location-based matching and our full in-person safety protocol, including a required emergency contact before the session is confirmed. Every session is tagged by modality so the right protections apply automatically, every time.

📍

On-Demand Geo-Matching

For in-person sessions, we start by searching a 3-mile radius and expand by 2 miles every 5 minutes — up to a 15-mile cap — until a match is found. Virtual sessions skip this entirely: they're matched instantly by subject and availability, regardless of where you or your tutor are located. One app, two very different ways to get help fast.

Safety, Built Into the Booking Flow

In-person sessions carry different risks than virtual ones — so we treat them differently, not identically.

Mandatory Emergency Contact

Before any in-person session can be booked, students are required to designate an emergency contact. This step cannot be skipped — it's a prerequisite for confirming the session, not an optional add-on.

Identity & Background Screening

Tutors offering in-person sessions must complete identity verification and pass a background screening before approval. We're publishing full details on scope, provider, and renewal cadence in our Trust & Safety Center as this program is finalized — we'd rather be precise than vague about what's actually covered.

Modality-Specific Protections

Geo-matching, radius expansion, and in-person safety check-ins apply only to in-person sessions. Virtual sessions rely instead on encrypted video, optional recording, and chat moderation — matched instantly with no location logic involved.

Transparent Verification Status

Every tutor profile shows which checks have passed — enrollment, GPA review, or both — so students and parents can see exactly what "verified" means for that tutor, rather than relying on a single catch-all label.
