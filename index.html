Transparency

What "Verified" actually means

We'd rather be precise than impressive. Here's exactly how each check works today.

✅ Enrollment Verification

Confirmed through a third-party clearinghouse-style service. This check attests that a tutor is actively enrolled or holds a degree — it does not evaluate coursework or grades.

Enrollment: Confirmed

📄 Academic Performance Review

Since no external database can verify course-level GPA, tutors upload an unofficial transcript reviewed by our internal team against a 3.0+ threshold in matched subjects. 10% of tutors are randomly audited monthly with an official registrar transcript.

GPA: Internally Reviewed
Safety

In-person sessions have extra safeguards

Because meeting in person is a different commitment than a video call, we require additional steps before an in-person session is ever confirmed.

🛡️

Background Screening Required

Tutors offering in-person sessions must pass a background screening before their profile is eligible for in-person bookings. Full policy details are shared during onboarding.

📇

Emergency Contact Required to Book

Students must designate an emergency contact before an in-person session can be confirmed — this step is mandatory, not optional, and cannot be skipped at checkout.

📍

Location-Based Matching

In-person sessions use radius-based matching that expands gradually, so tutors and students are matched within a reasonable, verifiable distance.

🎥

Virtual Sessions, Different Protections

Virtual sessions don't require geo-matching or physical safety checks — instead they run through encrypted video with optional recording and moderated chat.
