Core Experience

Same-Campus Instant Match

Our matching engine connects your session request exclusively with verified tutors enrolled at your own campus, based on subject, availability, and rating. This is the default experience across every launched campus — not a fallback, the main event.

Transparent Fallback
📡

SLA-Backed Nearby-Campus Fallback

No same-campus tutor accepts within 10 minutes? We disclose it immediately and auto-escalate to a verified nearby-campus video tutor for up to 5 more minutes. Still unmatched at 15 minutes? You get a $5 session credit and a guaranteed match within 30 minutes via priority queue.

ⓘ The 15-minute guarantee is only live on campuses that have cleared our minimum verified tutor density threshold. Below that, you'll see a real-time estimated wait instead — never a false promise.
