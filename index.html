Core Feature
⏱️

Smart Matching & Booking SLA

Post a session request — subject, format (in-person or video), and urgency — and get matched to a verified, available tutor. Our pilot targets a median match time under 2 hours, backed by real tutor-supply data from campus recruitment drives, not an inflated promise.

Core Feature
💸

Tiered, Subject-Based Pricing

No more one-size-fits-all $20/hr. Rates scale with subject complexity and tutor credentials across three transparent tiers, with a fixed 10% commission and take-home pay shown before any tutor accepts a booking.

Core Feature

Lightweight Verified Tutors

Launch verification means .edu confirmation plus transcript or grade-proof review within 24–48 hours — fast enough for peer tutoring, with full background checks planned as we scale in Phase 2.
