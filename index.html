Why QuadMatch

Built for the way students actually study

Three systems working together: instant local matching, an honest fallback, and a verification model tutors can actually afford.

Core Experience
📍

Same-Campus Instant Match

Our matching engine connects your session request exclusively with verified tutors enrolled at your own campus — filtered by subject, availability, and rating. This is the default experience in 100% of launched campuses.

Safety Net
🛟

SLA-Backed Fallback, Fully Disclosed

No local match in 10 minutes? We auto-escalate to verified nearby-campus video tutors for 5 more minutes — clearly disclosed, never hidden. Still unmatched at 15 minutes? You get a $5 credit and a guaranteed match within 30 minutes. The hard 15-minute guarantee only activates once a campus clears our verified tutor density threshold; otherwise you see a real-time estimated wait.

Tutor Trust
✅

Fair, Recoverable Verification

Background checks cost $25 — tutors pay just $10 upfront, we front the rest. The full $25 comes back as earnings credit after 5 paid sessions in 60 days. Tutors set their own rate ($15–$30/hr), and our platform fee scales with session value (8%, floor $1.50, cap $3) instead of eating into higher-priced sessions.
