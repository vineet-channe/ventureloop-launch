Why TutorVerify

Built on Trust, Designed for Students

Every tutor on our platform goes through a rigorous, multi-step verification pipeline before they ever meet a student.

🔍

Verification & Background Check Pipeline

Government ID liveness checks, transcript verification against registrar/NSC records, national + county background checks via Checkr (refreshed every 12 months), and manual Trust & Safety review of any flagged mismatch within 48 hours. Only fully-verified tutors earn the badge and can list in-person sessions.

🛡️

Safety & Trust Toolkit

E-signed liability waivers before any first in-person booking, GPS check-in/check-out with 15-minute no-show auto-flagging, an in-app SOS button linked to campus security and 911, and $1M tutor liability micro-insurance built into every in-person session — no extra cost to students.

🏛️

Institutional Partner Dashboard

A compliance center for university administrators with real-time verification status, insurance certificates, FERPA-compliant data-sharing logs, and incident tracking. Institutions can gate access to verified + insured tutors only, and unlock co-branded pages with bulk licensing.

Simple, Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, no surprise charges. Students pay only for their session — payments are held in escrow and released after each verified session completes.
